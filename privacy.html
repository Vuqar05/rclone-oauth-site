Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Application Scope

rclone vugar is a personal-use application only. It is a command-line configuration of the open-source rclone tool, used by a single individual to sync and manage their own files between their local device and their own Google Drive account. It is not distributed to, or intended for use by, the general public, and it has no separate servers, backend, or hosted infrastructure of its own — all rclone operations run locally on the user's own device.

Information We Collect

rclone vugar does not operate any web service, server, or database. When you authorize this application, Google issues OAuth access and refresh tokens directly to the local rclone process running on your device. The application itself does not collect, receive, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or other Google account profile information, beyond what is needed to complete the OAuth handshake
  • The contents of the files stored in your Google Drive
  • Usage analytics, device identifiers, IP addresses, or browsing behavior

The only data involved is the OAuth token itself, which is generated by Google and stored locally on your own device by the rclone configuration file (typically rclone.conf). This token never leaves your device except to communicate directly with Google's own APIs.

OAuth Scopes Requested and Why

This application requests access to your Google account using the following OAuth scopes, each of which is required for a specific rclone file-management feature:

  • drive — Full access to view, create, modify, and delete files and folders in your Google Drive. Required so that rclone can perform two-way sync, upload, download, rename, move, and delete operations that you explicitly initiate from the command line.
  • drive.metadata.readonly — Read-only access to file and folder metadata (names, sizes, checksums, modification timestamps). Required so that rclone can compare local and remote files to decide what needs to be synced without re-downloading unchanged files.
  • docs — Read-only access to Google Docs metadata. Required so that rclone can correctly list and export native Google Docs/Sheets/Slides files that are stored in your Drive alongside regular files.

No scope is used for any purpose beyond the local file-synchronization operations you directly initiate.

How Your Data Is Used

Your Google Drive data is never stored, viewed, shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party. All file transfers happen directly between the rclone process on your own device and Google's Drive API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. This application does not maintain any external database, server-side log files, or persistent copies of your files or account activity. The developer of this application has no access to your files, your Google account, or your OAuth tokens at any time.

Data Retention and Deletion

The only credential associated with this application — your OAuth token — is stored locally on your own device inside your rclone configuration file, under your sole control. You can delete it at any time by removing the corresponding entry from rclone.conf or by running rclone config delete. You can also revoke this application's access entirely, at any time, from your Google Account's third-party access settings. No copies of this token, or of your Drive data, are retained anywhere else.

Data Sharing

This application does not share any data with third parties. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers involved in any part of this application.

No Tracking or Analytics

This application contains no tracking scripts, cookies, analytics, or telemetry. Your usage is completely private.

Children's Privacy

This application is intended solely for personal use by its individual developer and is not directed at, marketed to, or knowingly used by children.

Your Rights

Because this is a personal-use application with no external data store, you always retain full control of your data: you can revoke OAuth access at any time via your Google Account settings, and you can delete the locally stored token at any time from your own device. No request to the developer is required to exercise these rights.
