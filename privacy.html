Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Application Scope

My rclone Client is a personal-use application only. It is designed for individual users who wish to manage their Google Drive files via the command-line using rclone.

Data Access

This application requests access to your Google Drive account using the following OAuth scopes:

  • drive — Read, write, and delete access to files and folders in your Google Drive
  • drive.metadata.readonly — Read-only access to file and folder metadata (names, sizes, timestamps, etc.)
  • docs — Access to Google Docs for metadata retrieval

These scopes are necessary only for performing file synchronization and management operations between your local device and Google Drive.

Data Handling

Your data is never stored, shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party. All file transfers occur directly between your device and Google Drive. This application does not maintain any database, log files, or persistent records of your files or activities.

No Tracking or Analytics

This application contains no tracking scripts, cookies, analytics, or telemetry. Your usage is completely private.
